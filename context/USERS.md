## Jobs

**JOB-01:** When I contribute a presentation section, I want to know the
evidence and integration standards, so I can deliver usable work without
causing unexpected repairs.
Evidence: INT-02 (Daniel left out expected citations and pricing because
completion was undefined). INT-01 (Maya found active-looking sections
that still needed evidence and integration repairs).

**JOB-02:** When my team approaches a deadline, I want to see risks and
assign responsibility early, so we can protect rehearsal without
burdening early finishers.
Evidence: INT-01 (Maya found incomplete sections near the deadline,
made unassigned repairs, and lost rehearsal time). INT-02 (Daniel's gaps
became visible too late for normal feedback).

## Profiles

### Profile 1: Early-finishing integrator (INT-01)
- **Situation:** *Reported (INT-01):* Maya finishes her part early and
  checks whether the full presentation fits together. *Assumed:* she
  encounters this pattern across multiple courses.
- **Job:** *Reported:* she needs to identify gaps early enough for the
  owner to respond. *Assumed:* she wants repair ownership recorded for
  later workload discussions.
- **Current approach:** *Reported:* she monitors the deck, messages
  teammates, and repairs gaps when time is short. *Assumed:* she treats
  slide activity as a progress signal.
- **Why unsatisfying:** *Reported:* activity conceals gaps, early
  finishers become editors, and rehearsal disappears. *Assumed:*
  repeated correction may damage trust.

### Profile 2: Time-constrained section owner (INT-02)
- **Situation:** *Reported (INT-02):* Daniel balances coursework with
  part-time shifts and works in limited time blocks. *Assumed:*
  asynchronous clarification suits him better than extra meetings.
- **Job:** *Reported:* he needs clear expectations and feedback before
  failure. *Assumed:* he wants to request review without appearing
  unreliable.
- **Current approach:** *Reported:* he interprets requirements,
  researches, edits slides, and uses group messages. *Assumed:* he
  would use a checklist attached to his section.
- **Why unsatisfying:** *Reported:* invisible research, vague
  expectations, and late feedback waste effort. *Assumed:* replacement
  reduces his opportunity to learn through revision.
